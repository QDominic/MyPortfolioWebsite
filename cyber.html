  Cybersecurity

Stay Safe in the Digital World

In 2026, cyber threats are more sophisticated than ever. Your habits and awareness are your strongest defence.

Critical Warning

Cybersecurity is no longer just an IT concern — it is a critical requirement for survival in 2026 due to the massive scale of digital crime and the rising sophistication of attackers including Romance Scams, Mobile Money Fraud, and WhatsApp Impersonation.

Common Cyber Threats

Romance Scams

Fraudsters build fake online relationships to gain your trust, then manipulate you into sending money. Never send money to someone you've only met online.

Mobile Money Fraud

Criminals impersonate telecom agents or use social engineering to steal your mobile money PIN. Never share your PIN or OTP with anyone, ever.

WhatsApp Impersonation

Attackers clone contacts or hack accounts to request urgent money transfers. Always verify through a phone call before sending any funds.

Phishing Attacks

Fake emails, SMS, and websites trick you into entering your credentials. Always check the URL and sender address before clicking any link.

Malicious Links

A single click on an untrusted link can install malware or grant attackers full access to your device. Think before you click — always.

Identity Theft

Your personal data is valuable. Criminals use stolen information to open accounts or commit fraud in your name. Guard your data fiercely.

Avoid the mindset that security is a "one-and-done" task. It's a constant habit of verifying everything and assuming that at some point, a defence will fail.

Zero Trust Security Principle

Ghana's Cybersecurity Authority

The Cyber Security Authority (CSA) of Ghana was established by the Cybersecurity Act, 2020 (Act 1038) to regulate cybersecurity activities in Ghana, promote the development of cybersecurity in the country, and provide for related matters.

Protection of Children Online for a Safer Digital Ghana is very important because our children are the future leaders. Report any online abuse or exploitation to the CSA Ghana immediately.
